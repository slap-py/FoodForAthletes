How it works
✍️
You describe the mealWrite it as you'd say it out loud — "big bowl of oatmeal with peanut butter and a banana." No special format required.
🤖
A model estimates the nutritionDayplate sends your description to an AI model trained on nutrition databases and portion research. It identifies each food, infers a likely portion, and returns the macros and micros.
✏️
You review and correctBefore saving, you see every food item and its estimated portion. Tap the edit button on any line to re-describe it — the model re-estimates that item instantly.
📸
Photos and voice add contextA photo of your plate, the packaging, or a nutrition label gives the model more to work with. Voice input is transcribed and treated as text. Either can be used alone or alongside a written description.
On accuracy: Estimates vary — restaurant portions, home cooking, and packaging all introduce uncertainty. Dayplate shows you what it assumed so you can correct anything that's off. Over time, your corrections make the log more useful.
How your data is handled
Plain language, no hidden tracking.
☁️
Meal descriptions leave your deviceWhen you log a meal, the text you wrote is sent to an AI model to estimate nutrition. This is the only time your description leaves your device. The text is not stored by the model provider after processing.
📸
Photos are not storedA photo you take is sent to the model for the single purpose of identifying the food. It is not saved anywhere — not on our servers, not in your log.
📱
Your log lives on your deviceAll saved meals are stored locally and optionally synced to your private iCloud. Dayplate has no server-side meal database for your account.
🚫
No selling, no adsDayplate does not sell your data, does not run ads, and does not share your meal history with third parties. The app is supported by a direct purchase or subscription — your data is not the product.
🗑️
You control deletionYou can export your full history as CSV or JSON, or delete all data from Settings → Account at any time. Deletion is permanent and immediate.
